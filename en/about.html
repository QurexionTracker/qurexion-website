Why QUREXION exists

Most traders know how much they won or lost – but not why. Which time of day, which coin, which leverage, which mood costs money? That stays invisible in exchange apps and spreadsheets.

QUREXION fetches your trades from the exchange automatically, analyses them and shows you honestly where your edge is and where you lose money. QUREXION calculates the numbers itself from your real trades – the AI only phrases the answer and never makes up results.

How we protect your account

Read-only keys onlyYou connect your exchange with a read-only key. Where the exchange allows the check, QUREXION automatically rejects keys with trading or withdrawal rights.
Stored encryptedExchange keys are stored encrypted and only sent to the exchange itself. In the Windows app, trades and keys stay on your PC.
Two-factor sign-inProtect your account with an authenticator app. Plus an anti-phishing code in our emails and a warning when you sign in from a new device.
Signed updatesThe Windows app only installs digitally signed, official versions. You can report security issues to us – contact in our security.txt.
