FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a trading journal – and why do I need one?

A journal records every trade with its result, setup and thoughts. Only then can you see which patterns make you money and which cost you money. QUREXION does the logging for you automatically.

Which exchanges are supported?

Bitget, Bitunix, Binance, Bybit, OKX and Hyperliquid via API. Other exchanges can be added via CSV import.

Can QUREXION trade on my behalf?

No. You connect your exchange with a read-only key. QUREXION can only read trades and positions – it can’t place orders or withdraw anything.

Where is my data stored?

In the Windows app on your PC – optionally with cloud sync to your account. In the web app in your account (Supabase, Frankfurt); exchange keys are stored encrypted there and only sent to the exchange. For prices and market data QUREXION uses public exchange APIs. For AI coach answers QUREXION only sends the calculated metrics for your question to QUREXION AI – if you prefer not to, switch to the local AI in the settings.

Windows warns during installation – is that normal?

Yes. Windows SmartScreen warns about new programs that haven’t been downloaded often yet or don’t have an expensive publisher certificate. Here’s how to run the setup anyway:

  1. In the blue “Windows protected your PC” window, click More info.
  2. At the bottom, click Run anyway.
  3. The setup starts normally.

Only download QUREXION from this site. Updates then arrive automatically and are digitally signed – QUREXION only installs official versions.

Does QUREXION run on Mac or mobile?

Yes – the web app at app.qurexion.com runs in any modern browser, including Mac, tablet and phone. On your phone you can add it to your home screen. The desktop app is available for Windows.

Does QUREXION help with taxes?

QUREXION exports all closed trades of a year in USD and EUR (ECB reference rate on the closing day) as a spreadsheet and report. This is not tax advice – please review it with your tax advisor.

How much does QUREXION cost?

You try QUREXION free for 14 days – no payment details. After that, QUREXION Pro costs €14.99 per month or €119 per year (incl. VAT). You can cancel anytime, effective at the end of the period.

Why do I need an account?

You use your account to sign in to the web app and the Windows app, and it checks whether your trial or subscription is active. The Windows app keeps working offline for up to 7 days.

Is QUREXION available in the browser?

Yes – at app.qurexion.com, with every feature: exchange sync, trade review, reports, alerts and AI coach. With cloud sync you see the same data in the browser and the Windows app.
